# Caffeine and Sleep Onset

## Introduction

- A randomised trial of 312 adults found a 41-minute delay. (p. 12)
- A second trial replicated the delay in 210 adults. (table 2)
- Sleep onset was 28 minutes later in the caffeine arm. (p. 4)

## Results
