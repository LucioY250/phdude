---
description: List research gaps - questions, claims, sources, artifacts and conflicts that need attention.
allowed-tools: Bash(phdude:*), Bash(npx:*)
phdude-managed: true
---

Arguments: `$ARGUMENTS`

Run:

```
phdude gaps $ARGUMENTS --json
```

Follow `.phdude/skills/literature/SKILL.md` to interpret the result: work high-severity gaps
first, propose evidence or claims through the CLI, and never invent a source to fill a gap - if
a gap needs one, say what to look for and ask the researcher.
